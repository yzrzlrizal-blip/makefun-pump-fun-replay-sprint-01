Breakout Rider: a pump.fun replay paper bot that waits for a coin to prove buying pressure — price up 30% from its first print, off its own low, real SOL in the curve, real trades printed — then rides the breakout with a small 0.04 SOL stake and exits fast on a stop-loss, a give-back from the peak, or a max hold. Sells are chunked to at most half of the curve's real SOL so a dump cannot trap the position.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
